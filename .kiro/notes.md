# Kiro Usage Notes

I used Kiro as a support tool during the development of Tarka, primarily to speed up iteration and clarify my thinking.

Specifically, Kiro helped me:
- Understand the intent of the Week 6 “The Referee” challenge
- Break the problem into clear decision points instead of jumping to a single answer
- Sketch initial logic for comparing AWS compute options
- Refine the way trade-offs are explained so they are easier to reason about

The final decisions, structure, and implementation were driven by my own judgment, with Kiro acting as an accelerator rather than a replacement for manual development.
